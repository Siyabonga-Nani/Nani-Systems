# Nani Systems Master Specification

## 1. What Nani Systems Is
Nani Systems is a technology-focused company serving:
- Individuals
- Upcoming entrepreneurs
- Startups
- Small businesses
- Established businesses
- People developing projects before formally turning them into businesses

*Note: A customer does NOT need to already have a registered business. A customer does need to be able to financially commit to the requested work.*

## 2. What Nani Systems Currently Offers
### Core Customer Promise
Nani Systems allows a customer to come with:
- An idea
- A partially developed idea
- An existing website
- An existing application
- An existing software project
- A problem
- A feature request
- A project requiring development
- A project requiring improvement
- A project requiring hosting
- A project requiring maintenance
- A technology problem requiring assistance

Nani Systems helps the customer determine the appropriate technological solution. Customers do not need to have everything figured out before approaching Nani Systems.

### Fundamental Service Model
Nani Systems is structured around three fundamental service categories:

**BUILD**
Technology built from the ground up.
*Examples:* Websites, Web applications, Mobile applications, Desktop software, Games, Custom software, MVPs, Prototypes, APIs, Integrations, Other approved technology projects.

**IMPROVE**
Improvement or modification of existing technology.
*Examples:* Feature additions, Bug fixing, Redesigns, Integrations, Payment integration, Authentication, Dashboards, APIs, Database work, Performance improvements, Migrations, Upgrades, Existing-project development.

**MANAGE**
Ongoing technical services.
*Examples:* Hosting, Maintenance, Security updates, Backups, Monitoring, Technical support, Ongoing development.

### Customer Flexibility
Customers may purchase one service, combine multiple services, build a project in stages, request improvements/maintenance/hosting/support/consultations, and return later for additional work. Customers are not required to use Nani Systems for every part of their technology infrastructure.

### Consultations
Nani Systems may provide consultations separately from development. Customers may communicate through normal channels or reserve paid consultation time when they require dedicated assistance.

## 3. Approved Business Rules

### Project Discovery & Acceptance
- Nani Systems helps clarify ideas, discuss approaches, recommend solutions, and adjust scope.
- Nani Systems may decline projects that are beyond current technical capability, beyond current operational capacity, too large for the company's current stage, technically unsuitable, or otherwise outside approved scope.
- Nani Systems must not pretend to have capabilities it does not currently possess.
- Nani Systems must communicate honestly if an existing project is too fragmented or technically unsuitable to continue efficiently, and may recommend rebuilding.

### Commercial Principle
- **Core Founder Rule: "PAYMENT IS COMMITMENT."**
- Nani Systems focuses operational resources on customers who have actually committed financially. A person expressing interest is not automatically a customer. A paying/committed customer receives priority.

### Scope Control Rule
- Every new requirement must be classified as: **NOW**, **NEXT**, or **REMOVED / NEVER**.
- If a proposed feature is not necessary for the current objective or first revenue-generating release, it should normally be moved to NEXT. Do not expand scope merely because a feature is interesting.

### Pricing Philosophy
- Pricing is scope-based. Public prices may use "from" pricing (e.g., website/application development from an approved starting price).
- The public starting price is NOT automatically the final project price.
- Final pricing depends on requirements, scope, features, complexity, development effort, integrations, infrastructure, and other project-specific requirements.

### Payment Philosophy
- Supported payment methods: full payment, milestone payments, monthly development payment plans.
- A minimum 20% financial commitment is required before a project is formally committed/reserved.

### Project Pausing
- A customer may pause a project, but a paused project does not remain indefinitely in the active development pipeline.
- After the applicable pause period, the project may leave the active pipeline.
- The customer may later need to pay a reservation/re-entry amount to place the project back into the active development pipeline.

### Customer Account & Dashboard
- Customers who proceed seriously will have a Nani Systems account and customer dashboard.
- The long-term concept includes: profile, projects, quotes, payments, payment plans, project status, history, files, support, maintenance, hosting, services, and future quotes. Customers can create additional requests from their dashboard.

### Ownership & Control
- Customers retain control of their own business-critical information and accounts (domains, hosting, payments, APIs, business data).
- Nani Systems provides technology development and management services, but does not take ownership of or run the customer's business.
- Nani Systems may manage customer infrastructure when explicitly authorised.

### Intellectual Property Philosophy
- Customer-specific commissioned deliverables belong to the customer where agreed.
- Nani Systems retains ownership of its internal technology, reusable frameworks, templates, internal engines, general-purpose systems, background IP, and reusable development technology.
- If a customer explicitly commissions exclusive custom technology for ownership, the arrangement must be explicitly defined for that project. A customer receiving a product built using internal tech does not automatically receive ownership of that underlying tech.

### Hosting, Maintenance, and Support
- **Hosting is optional.** Customers may use Nani Systems, use another provider, keep their existing provider, move elsewhere, or return later for migration. Hosting is a technology management/service offering, not merely raw server space.
- **Maintenance is optional.** Customers without an ongoing maintenance/support service are not entitled to unlimited free technical work after completion. A post-launch defect period may apply. After this period, Nani Systems' implementation defects are handled per project terms, while customer-caused changes/new requirements are paid work unless covered by a service.

## 4. What is Explicitly Out of Scope
The current launch scope focuses strictly on **TECHNOLOGY** (building, improving, deploying, hosting, maintaining, and supporting customer technology).

The following are explicitly **out of scope** for the current launch:
- General business consulting
- Business restructuring
- Accounting
- Legal services
- General business management

*(These may be reconsidered in the future.)*

## 5. What Belongs to Future Phases
The exact implementation and mechanics of various business rules belong to later development phases. Do not implement these during Phase 0:
- The exact pricing engine implementation.
- Exact mechanics of milestone payments, reservation components, credits, refunds, payment plans, and project continuation.
- Implementation of the paused project workflow and re-entry.
- Technical implementation of the customer account and dashboard.
- Detailed legal and intellectual property terms.
- Detailed infrastructure implementation for hosting.
- Exact warranty and support mechanics.
- Consultation pricing and scheduling mechanics.
- Large enterprise-scale projects (may be considered later as the company grows).
